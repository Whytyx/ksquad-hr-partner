About

Hi, I'm Kevalin Kitiyanuphap.

I help hospitality owners and managers with the people side of the business: how you hire, how someone starts, how a shift is led, and how the hard conversations get handled before they turn into resignations or formal problems.

KSquad HR Partner is a consultancy, not a recruitment agency and not a call centre. The work stays close to the floor. Clearer roles, fairer habits, and managers who feel able to lead. You keep the decisions. I help you make them with less noise.

I am not interested in importing a corporate handbook into a restaurant or a hotel and calling that HR. If a document will not be used on a Saturday night, it is the wrong document.

How I work

A few things you can expect.

Direct

I will tell you what I think is going on, including when the answer is not an HR project.

Discreet

People matters stay between us and the colleagues who need to be involved. I do not collect case studies to publish.

Practical

Advice you can try on the next rota, not a model that needs a department to run it.
