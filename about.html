About

Hi, I'm Kevalin Kitiyanuphap.

I've spent over 13 years inside a growing hospitality business — starting in operations, moving through HR and operations, and ultimately leading the people function as Head of HR.

That means I don't just know employment processes on paper. I know what it's like to run a service, lose a chef on a Friday, hire at volume, manage sponsored workers, and keep a business compliant while it's still growing.

Over that time I've handled everything from high-volume recruitment and Right to Work compliance to complex investigations, sponsor licence duties, policy overhauls and supporting managers through some genuinely difficult situations.

How I work

  • Straight talking — no jargon, no hedging, no 40-page reports nobody reads
  • Practical first — advice you can act on this week
  • Commercially realistic — I understand that the business still has to trade
  • Confidential and discreet — always

I set up KSquad HR Partner because small and growing businesses deserve the same quality of people support that larger companies pay a full-time team for — without the cost of one.
