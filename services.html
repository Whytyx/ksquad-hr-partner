Services

How the work actually runs.

You stay in charge of the business. I help you put clearer people habits in place, then stay close enough that they get used.

See how it works

  1. A first conversation

    We look at what is actually getting in the way: a role you cannot fill, a manager who is stuck, or a pattern of people leaving. You do not need a briefing pack.

  2. A clear scope

    If we work together, we agree what I will do, what you will do, and what “better” looks like. No open-ended retainer disguised as a project.

  3. Hands-on support

    That might be a hiring process, a first-month plan, a difficult conversation rehearsed properly, or a simple way for managers to handle repeat questions.

  4. A review

    We check what changed on the floor, not only what was written down, and decide what should happen next.

The work

Four ways I can help.

Hiring and onboarding

Role clarity before you advertise. A short, fair process. A first month that tells a new person what good looks like, who to ask, and how the place actually runs.

Everyday people questions

Absence, conduct, pay questions that have become personal, and the conversations managers postpone. I help you handle them calmly and consistently. This is not a law firm. Where a question needs a lawyer, I will say so.

Manager support

For supervisors who are excellent at service and newer to leading people: feedback, fairness on the rota, and how to have a hard conversation without losing the room.

Ongoing partnership

A regular rhythm of support when you want a people partner on hand and do not need, or cannot yet justify, a full-time HR hire.

What this is not

  • Not a recruitment agency. I do not fill a vacancy and disappear after the start date.
  • Not a generic HR hotline. The advice is for your operation, not a script written for every industry.
  • Not software. No portal to log into. The work is conversations, decisions, and simple tools your team will use.
