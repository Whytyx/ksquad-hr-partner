Services

Whether you need one problem solved or ongoing support, I work in a way that fits how your business actually runs.

Hiring & Onboarding

From vacancy to ready-to-work.

Hiring isn't finished when someone accepts the job. It's finished when they're legally checked, properly contracted, set up on your systems and ready for their first shift. I can manage that whole process — or just the parts you don't have time for.

What's included:

  • Vacancy planning and job descriptions
  • Advertising and candidate sourcing
  • CV screening and shortlisting
  • Interview coordination and interview support
  • Offer management and references
  • Right to Work checks and record-keeping
  • Employment contracts and new starter documentation
  • Onboarding process and employee records setup
  • Probation setup and review framework

The difference: A recruitment agency sends you CVs. I make sure the person you hire is compliantly employed and genuinely ready to start.

People Compliance

Know what you need. Fix what's missing. Reduce your people risk.

Most small businesses aren't non-compliant on purpose — they're just busy, and nobody's checked in a while. I'll review what you have, tell you plainly where the gaps are, and help you fix them in priority order.

What's included:

  • People/HR compliance audit with a prioritised action plan
  • Right to Work audits and ongoing check processes
  • Employment contracts and statements of particulars
  • Employee handbook and HR policies
  • Employee files, records and documentation
  • Working Time, holiday, sickness and absence processes
  • Probation and performance frameworks
  • Maternity, paternity and family leave processes
  • Sexual harassment prevention duty — processes and risk assessment
  • Employment law change reviews and updates
  • Sponsor licence and sponsored worker compliance support
  • Manager guidance so your team knows what to do

Employee Relations & Investigations

Practical support when people problems become difficult.

Getting this wrong is expensive — in tribunal risk, in management time, and in team morale. I can guide you through it, or take the whole process off your hands.

What's included:

  • Disciplinary processes and hearings
  • Grievances and appeals
  • Workplace investigations (conducted independently)
  • Performance and capability management
  • Probation concerns and exits
  • Short-term and long-term sickness absence
  • Conduct concerns and workplace conflict
  • Suspension and process guidance
  • Hearing preparation and outcome documentation
  • Manager coaching through difficult cases

Works either way: "I've got a situation and I don't know what to do" → advice and documents. Or "Please just handle this" → I run the process end to end.

Ongoing People Support

Your people support, without needing an in-house HR team.

For businesses with employees who don't yet need a full-time HR Manager — but do need someone to call when something comes up.

What's included:

  • Day-to-day people and employment queries
  • New starters, leavers and changes
  • Contracts, letters and documentation
  • Recruitment and onboarding support
  • Absence, probation and performance
  • Employee issues as they arise
  • Periodic compliance checks and reminders
  • Policy updates when the law changes
  • Manager support and guidance

Available as monthly support or as-and-when — depending on how much is going on in your business.

FAQs

Do I need to be a certain size to work with you? No. I work with businesses from a handful of employees upwards.

Do you only work with hospitality? No. Hospitality is where my experience is deepest, but the support works for any growing business.

Are you a solicitor? No. I provide practical HR and people support based on employment law and best practice. Where something needs formal legal or immigration advice, I'll tell you and can work alongside your adviser.

How much does it cost? It depends on what you need. After an initial conversation I'll give you a clear price — fixed fee, day rate or monthly support.

Can you just do a one-off piece of work? Yes. Plenty of clients start with one audit or one issue.

Not sure which one you need?

Most enquiries start with "something's come up." Tell me what's happening and we'll work it out.
