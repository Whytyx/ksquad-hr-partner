# KSquad HR Partner

Multi-page marketing site for KSquad HR Partner, a hospitality HR consultancy.

Live: https://whytyx.github.io/ksquad-hr-partner/

Pages: Home, Services, Hospitality, About, Contact, plus short placeholder Privacy, Cookies, and Terms pages.

The contact form is static. It opens a mailto to hello@example.com (a placeholder address) and shows an on-page confirmation. Company number and address are not published yet: the footer says “Company details to follow”.
