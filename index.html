Where it usually strains

The same three pressures, most weeks.

Hiring in a rush

A role opens, the floor is short, and the process becomes whoever can start soonest. Standards slip before the person has even learned the place.

Managers left to guess

Supervisors are promoted because they are good at service, then handed people problems with no shared way to handle them.

Good people leaving

Turnover gets treated as normal for hospitality. Sometimes it is the rota, the start, or a conversation that never happened.

What I help with

Support you can use on a Friday, not a folder for a shelf.

  • Hiring and onboarding. Clear roles, a fair process, and a first month that does not depend on whoever is on shift.
  • Everyday people questions. Absence, conduct, and the conversations that get delayed until they are bigger.
  • Manager support. Practical help for people who lead a team as well as a service.
  • A steady partnership. Ongoing support if you want a people partner without hiring a full-time HR team.

Questions

Before you get in touch.

Is this a recruitment agency?

No. A recruitment agency fills a vacancy and moves on. KSquad HR Partner is a consultancy: how you hire, how people start, how managers lead, and how you handle what comes up after someone joins.

Who is it for?

Independent hotels, restaurants, cafés, bars, and small hospitality groups. It is especially useful if you are still the person who answers the people questions yourself.

What happens in a free initial conversation?

We talk about the business, what is getting in the way, and whether working together is a good fit. There is no obligation and no prepared pitch.
